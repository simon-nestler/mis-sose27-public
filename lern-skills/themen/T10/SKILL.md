# T10 add-on · Accessibility: Law and Checking · v1.0 · August 2026

This add-on extends the module practice partner; all its rules keep applying. In this topic the dating rule is existential: this is the one session with a genuine memorization block, and models fail it constantly.

## The core conflict of this session

The checker finds 57, 40, or 14 percent of the barriers — depending on whom you believe — and the law does not ask which tool you had.

## Deepening tasks (built for resistance)

1. „Refute as strongly as you can: *Automated accessibility checkers are useless because they miss most barriers.* Use the fact that three different coverage numbers exist — and explain what each one actually measured before you use it."
2. „I claim a green axe/WAVE report is legally worthless under the EAA. Argue the strongest possible case AGAINST me — then name the FTC accessiBe order and tell me whether it damages or supports your case, and why."
3. „Steelman the accessibility-overlay industry: what real problem do overlays respond to, and where exactly does the Overlay Fact Sheet's objection bite anyway?"
4. „Quiz me on the fact block: EAA date, what it covers, BFSG enforcement body, WCAG version status, EN 301 549 status — one question at a time, and correct my dates mercilessly."

## The check question

Every time this assistant names a standard or a legal consequence: is there a date attached, and does it match the reader's fact box (session 10, „What you should know beforehand")? Two hard tripwires: any claim that WCAG 3.0 is the current standard is wrong (working draft, will not supersede WCAG 2), and any concrete German BFSG fine case is unverified as of August 2026 — ask the assistant for its source and watch it not have one.

## Exam-relevant here — and not

Relevant (column 1): the fact block itself (the semester's only memorization block: dates, documents, versions), the three-numbers format (seed K10-1), and the checker-limits format (K10-2, the LZ4 reference format). Not exam material: tool handling (Ü5 territory), lawsuit statistics, and anything about EN 301 549 V4.1.1 beyond „in transition — verify" until the OJEU citation is confirmed.
