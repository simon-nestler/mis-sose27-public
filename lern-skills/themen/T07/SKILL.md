# T07 add-on · Voice Dialogs: Written and Generated · v1.0 · August 2026

This add-on extends the module practice partner; all its rules keep applying — especially: no finished solutions, everything dated.

## The core conflict of this session

Voice dialogs are generated at runtime now, and the designer shapes a behavior they will never see in full.

## Deepening tasks (built for resistance — copy one at a time)

1. „Refute as strongly as you can: *The classic VUI concepts — confirmation strategies, barge-in, error prompts — are obsolete now that language models generate dialogs at runtime.* Then attack your own refutation once."
2. „I claim expectation management is now the number-one design task of conversational interfaces, above dialog structure. Argue against me using the Alexa+ launch history — then tell me which of your arguments would not survive the Consumer Reports findings."
3. „Steelman the position that a pharmacy refill dialog should NEVER be handled by a generated conversation. Then name the one design change that would weaken your own case most."
4. „Quiz me: give me a generated dialog-flow excerpt with exactly two plausible-but-wrong design decisions hidden in it, and let me find them. Do not reveal them until I commit to two answers."

## The check question

When this assistant tells you how voice assistants „should" be designed: which decade is the advice from? If it talks intents, slots, and skill invocation phrases as current practice, it is answering from the pre-LLM world — the counter-evidence is in the reader, session 7, „What you should know beforehand". Mind the distinction the session insists on: those concepts are historical as *practice* and current as *instruments* — intents, confirmation, confidence tiers and barge-in are what you test a generated dialog with, and nothing you use to catch these errors is newer than 2016.

## Exam-relevant here — and not

Relevant (column 1): the autopsy format on generated dialog flows (seed K07-1, LZ3 reference format) and the promise-vs-finding gap format (K07-2). Not exam material: writing dialog scripts yourself, tool-specific knowledge (that lives in exercise block Ü3), and the history of ELIZA to Mitsuku — reader background, never a question.
