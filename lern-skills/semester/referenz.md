## The nine learning objectives (numbers as used in the module)

LZ1 select and justify modality combinations for a context, naming the cost · LZ2 analyze breakdowns at modality transitions · LZ3 evaluate AI-generated interaction designs for plausible-but-wrong elements · LZ4 analyze accessibility of concrete multimodal systems, including checker limits · LZ5 propose modality substitutions and evaluate their side effects · LZ6 judge conversational vs. graphical vs. hybrid for a task · LZ7 diagnose failing gesture sets with named principles · LZ8 build and iteratively evaluate a multimodal prototype (exercise track) · LZ9 defend and revise design decisions in critique (exercise track). LZ1–LZ7 are exam-relevant (column 1); LZ8/LZ9 live in the exercises (column 2) — disclosed in T01.

## Working definitions (module versions)

**Modality:** a channel of human perception (vision, hearing, touch) or action (speech, gesture, gaze, manipulation) used for interaction. **Redundant / complementary / conflicting:** two channels carrying the same, dividing, or contradicting information (fusion/fission). **The decision procedure (T03):** users → environment → task → error consequences → choice with a named rejected alternative and a named price. **Articulation barrier (T08):** users who cannot verbalize their goal get no help from a prompt-only interface. **Plausible-but-wrong (T09):** an element that looks professional and fails in use; found via the four audit steps assumptions → states → edges → provenance. **Modality substitution (T11):** deliberately replacing or doubling a channel to remove a barrier — never free of side effects.

## Exam frame

Written exam, 90 minutes, AI excluded. Formats rehearsed in sessions: case vignette with justified choice (LZ1), breakdown analysis (LZ2), plausible-but-wrong autopsy (LZ3), checker-limits analysis (LZ4), substitution with side effect (LZ5), channel decision with named criteria (LZ6), gesture diagnosis (LZ7). A mock exam built from the semester's seeds is issued in T13. Bonus rule: six exercise submissions within 48 h each compensate 5 % of exam points.
