# How to use the practice partner (half a page, honestly)

**What this is.** Two files that turn the AI assistant of your choice into a practice partner for this module: it will quiz you, push back, and refuse to do your homework — which is the point. It contains nothing that is not also in the course reader. Using it is voluntary; nobody who works without it is at any disadvantage.

**How to install it.**

- *Tools that read skill files* (many current assistants and coding tools do): place the folder with `SKILL.md` and `referenz.md` where the tool looks for skills, or attach both files to your project/workspace.
- *Custom assistants with an instruction field* (custom GPTs, Gems, and similar): copy the full text of `SKILL.md` (below the `---` block) into the instruction field — it fits the usual limit — and upload `referenz.md` as a knowledge file.
- *Plain chat, no setup:* paste the text of `SKILL.md` as your first message, then upload or paste `referenz.md`. Repeat per conversation; models forget between chats.

**How to check it works.** Ask it for the finished solution to an exercise task. If it hands one over, the setup failed — reinstall or re-paste. A working partner answers with a question.

**Version:** v1.0, August 2026, built for summer term 2027. If your copy has no version line, it is not current — the latest version is on Moodle. Two session-specific add-ons (T07, T10) appear there during the semester.
